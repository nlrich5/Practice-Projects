The British Columbia Hockey Conference (BCHC) is an upcoming British Columbia-based Junior A ice hockey league administrated by BC Hockey. The league was formed in 2026 with 22 teams set to be competing in the league's first season in 2026–27.[1][2]

History

[edit]

BCHL departure from CJHL and Hockey Canada

[edit]
Main article: British Columbia Hockey League

In 2021, the British Columbia Hockey League (BCHL), the top Junior A league in British Columbia, announced their departure from the Canadian Junior Hockey League (CJHL).[3]

In May 2023, the BCHL announced that they would be splitting from Hockey Canada and become an independent league. The decision was made as the league was looking to attract 16- and 17-year-old players who were capable of playing Major Junior but wanted to retain their NCAA eligibility.[4] The BCHL's departure from Hockey Canada sanctioned competition meant that British Columbia was without a sanctioned Hockey Canada Junior A league.[5]

KIJHL and PJHL

[edit]

In 2022, the Kootenay International Junior Hockey League (KIJHL) and Pacific Junior Hockey League (PJHL), two Junior B leagues, formed an organization called the British Columbia Hockey Conference (BCHC) to coordinate a joint player safety department and a top prospects game between the two leagues.[6]

In December 2023, BC Hockey began the process of restructuring Junior A hockey in British Columbia. The three Junior B leagues (PJHL, KIJHL and VIJHL) were designated as "Junior A Tier 2", with plans to evaluate teams seeking to be promoted to "Junior A Tier 1".[7]

On March 18, 2025, the PJHL announced that for the 2025–26 season, the league would split into two, with seven teams in Junior A Tier 1 and eight in Junior A Tier 2.[8] This proposal was rejected by a vote of member clubs, but the league realigned its divisions along the lines of the proposed split.

Formation of league

[edit]

On April 21, 2026, the BC Hockey, along with the KIJHL and PJHL, announced the launch of the British Columbia Hockey Conference as a sanctioned Junior A league. The league will start in 2026–27, and feature 22 teams split into three divisions.[9][1] The KIJHL will cease to exist following the departure of 14 teams to the BCHC after losing eight teams to the new Western International Junior Hockey League (WIJHL) earlier in 2026.[10][11] The BCHC plans to apply for membership to the CJHL to bring British Columbia back into the national system of Junior A.[1]

Teams

[edit]

The 2026–27 BCHC season will feature 22 teams in three divisions.[1]

Future

[edit]

The Summerland Jets are expected to only play one season in the BCHC as they were only originally granted tier 2/junior B status[13], but was forced into the league by the eight other tier two teams creating the independent WIJHL. It is also expected that if the former KIJHL teams who left Hockey Canada wanted to rejoin the KIJHL, the door is open.[14]
